The Bureau's expansive and well-appointed office suite was taken over by the State Department Book Club, and now the agents have been consigned to a tiny suite in a strip mall in the remote D.C. suburb of Swamp Park, Maryland. :

4. 句子类型 (Sentence Type)

这是一个 **并列句 (Compound Sentence)**。
  • 解释: 并列句是由两个或多个简单句 (也叫独立分句，就是它们自己本身可以独立成为一个完整的句子) 用并列连词 (如 and, but, or, so 等) 连接而成的。
    • 这个句子里，"The Bureau's expansive and well-appointed office suite was taken over by the State Department Book Club" 是一个完整的简单句。
    • "now the agents have been consigned to a tiny suite in a strip mall in the remote D.C. suburb of Swamp Park, Maryland" 也是一个完整的简单句。
    • 它们通过并列连词 and 连接在一起。
