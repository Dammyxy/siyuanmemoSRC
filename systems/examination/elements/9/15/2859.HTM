Two segments are congruent if they have the same length.
若两条线段长度相同，则它们全等。

    • Angle bisector: 角平分线:

  • An angle bisector is a line that divides an angle into two congruent angles.
    角平分线是将一个角分成两个全等角的直线。

    • Midpoint: 中点:

  • The midpoint of a line segment is the point that splits it into two segments of equal length.
    线段的中点是将该线段分成两条等长部分的点。

    • Equilateral triangle: 等边三角形:

  • A triangle is equilateral if all three sides are congruent.
    若三角形的三条边均全等，则该三角形为等边三角形。

    • Isosceles triangle: 等腰三角形:

  • A triangle is isosceles if two (and only two) sides are congruent.
    若三角形有且仅有两条边全等，则该三角形为等腰三角形。

    • Right triangle: 直角三角形:

  • A triangle is right if one of its internal angles is a right angle.
    若一个三角形的内角中有一个直角，则该三角形为直角三角形。

    • Median: 中线:

  • A median of a triangle is a line segment joining a vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side.
    三角形的中线是连接一个顶点与对边中点的线段。

    • Altitude (or Height): 高 (或高度):

  • An altitude (height) of a triangle is a line segment drawn from one vertex of the triangle perpendicular to the opposite side (or the extension of the opposite side).
    三角形的高是从三角形的一个顶点向对边 (或对边的延长线) 所作的垂线段。

    • Perpendicular lines: 垂直线:

  • Two lines are perpendicular if they form a right angle at their intersection point.
    如果两条线在交点处形成直角，则这两条线互相垂直。

We'll also make use of the following:
我们还将利用以下性质:

    • The right angle postulate:
      直角公设:

  • All right angles are congruent.
    所有直角都全等。

  • The SSS, SAS, AAS, ASA, and HL congruence criteria for triangles.
    三角形全等的 SSS (边边边)、SAS (边角边)、AAS (角角边)、ASA (角边角) 和 HL (斜边直角边) 判定准则。
  • The reflexive, symmetric, and transitive properties of equality and congruence.
    等式与全等关系的自反性、对称性和传递性性质。
