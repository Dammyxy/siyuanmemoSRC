: The Perpendicular Bisector Theorems (垂直平分线定理)

The diagram below shows a segment ―AB and a line ↔CD, where ↔CD is the perpendicular bisector of ―AB.
下图显示了一条线段 ―AB 和一条直线 ↔CD，其中 ↔CD 是 ―AB 的垂直平分线。

We have already seen two theorems about perpendicular bisectors. We state them below:
我们已经见过关于垂直平分线的两个定理。我们将其陈述如下：

  • The perpendicular bisector theorem (垂直平分线定理):

    If a point lies on the perpendicular bisector of a segment, then the point is equidistant from the segment's endpoints.
    如果一个点在一条线段的垂直平分线上，那么这个点到线段两个端点的距离相等。

     

    In other words, if a point lies on the perpendicular bisector of a segment, this guarantees that the point is the same distance from the segment's endpoints.
    换句话说，如果一个点在线段的垂直平分线上，这保证了该点到线段两个端点的距离相同。
  • The converse perpendicular bisector theorem (垂直平分线逆定理):

    If a point is equidistant from the endpoints of a segment, then it lies on the perpendicular bisector of the segment.
    如果一个点到一条线段两个端点的距离相等，那么这个点在这条线段的垂直平分线上。

     

    In other words, if a point is at the same distance from a segment's endpoints, this guarantees that the point lies on the perpendicular bisector of the segment.
    换句话说，如果一个点到线段两个端点的距离相同，这保证了该点位于线段的垂直平分线上。

In this lesson, we'll learn some proofs of the perpendicular bisector theorem. In a future lesson, we'll prove its converse.
在这一课中，我们将学习垂直平分线定理的一些证明。在未来的一课中，我们将证明它的逆定理。

Before we begin, note that in addition to the tools we've already used, we'll need to use the following:
在我们开始之前，请注意，除了我们已经使用过的工具外，我们还需要使用以下内容：

  • The definition of a bisector (平分线的定义):

    A bisector of a segment is a segment, line, or ray that bisects the segment.
    一条线段的平分线是平分该线段的另一条线段、直线或射线。

     

  • The definition of a perpendicular bisector (垂直平分线的定义):

    A perpendicular bisector of a segment is a segment, line, or ray that bisects the segment and is perpendicular to that segment.
    一条线段的垂直平分线是平分该线段并与其垂直的另一条线段、直线或射线。

     

  • The congruence criteria for triangles and CPCTC (三角形的全等判定准则和CPCTC).
  • The Pythagorean theorem (needed for an alternative proof) (勾股定理(用于备用证明))。
