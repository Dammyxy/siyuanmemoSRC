Title: 6. 举一反三 (Examples & Practice) : 句子1：Squinting up into the storm, you wonder how everything managed to happen so fast.

  • 用现在分词短语作状语 (像 "Squinting up...")：
    1. Smiling happily, the boy ran to his mother. (小男孩开心地笑着，跑向他的妈妈。)
    2. Feeling tired, she decided to take a short rest. (因为感觉累了，她决定稍作休息。)
  • 用宾语从句 (像 "how everything managed...")：
    1. I want to know what he is doing. (我想知道他在做什么。)
    2. She asked me if I could help her. (她问我是否能帮她。)
