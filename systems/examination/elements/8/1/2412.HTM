Where do ideas come from?
创意从何而来？

  • Powerful innovations often focus on creating new paradigms, not solving problems of the current context
    强大的创新往往着眼于创建新范式，而非解决当前情境中的问题
  • Insight through making  通过实践获得洞见
    • Effective system design requires insights drawn from serious contexts of use
      有效的系统设计需要从真实使用场景中汲取深刻见解
    • Insight through making prefers bricolage to big design up front
      通过实践获得的洞见更倾向于即兴创作而非预先的大规模设计
  • Evergreen note-writing helps insight accumulate
    常青笔记写作有助于洞见的积累
    • Brainstorming may often substitute for missing insight accretion systems
      头脑风暴常常可以替代缺失的洞见积累系统
  • Some kinds of creative insight depend on long-term memory
    某些类型的创造性洞见依赖于长期记忆
  • Idleness  闲散
    • “You get good ideas from years of hard leisure”; Quit Your Job - Wolf Tivy
      "好的创意来自多年的勤奋闲暇"；《辞职吧》——沃尔夫·蒂维
    • Dyson claims idleness is necessary to the best creative work
      戴森声称，懒散对于最出色的创造性工作必不可少
    • Kevin Kelly: “Efficiency is highly overrated; Goofing off is highly underrated. Regularly scheduled sabbaths, sabbaticals, vacations, breaks, aimless walks and time off are essential for top performance of any kind. The best work ethic requires a good rest ethic.”
      凯文·凯利："效率被严重高估；闲逛被严重低估。定期安排的安息日、学术休假、假期、休息、无目的散步和停工时间对于任何类型的顶尖表现都至关重要。最好的工作伦理需要良好的休息伦理。"
    • The Scientific Virtues from Slime Mold Time Mold — see “laziness”
      《黏菌时间模的科学美德》中提到的"懒惰"美德——参见"懒惰"章节

Knowledge workers usually have no specific methods for developing ideas over time
知识工作者通常缺乏随时间推移发展想法的具体方法

Skilled knowledge workers are expected to innovate—to produce valuable, original insight. If they have some inkling of an idea, how do they actually go about fleshing that idea out over time? What does taking a step look like? Where do ideas come from?
